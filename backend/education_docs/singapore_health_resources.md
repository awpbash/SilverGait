# Singapore Health Resources for Older Adults

Singapore has a comprehensive network of healthcare services, subsidies, and support programmes designed for older adults. Navigating these resources can be confusing, so this guide provides a clear overview of the key organisations, schemes, and services available to you. Understanding what is available can help you access the right care at the right time and reduce your out-of-pocket costs.

## Agency for Integrated Care (AIC)

The AIC is the government agency responsible for coordinating and facilitating community care services for older adults in Singapore. It is your one-stop resource for finding care services.

**AIC Silver Line: 1800-650-6060**
This free hotline connects you with information on:
- Home care and home medical services
- Day care and rehabilitation centres
- Nursing homes and residential care
- Caregiver support and training
- Assistive devices and home modifications
- Financial assistance for care services

**Key AIC Services:**
- **Care services directory:** An online directory of all community care providers, searchable by location and type of service.
- **Silver Generation Office (SGO):** AIC ambassadors who visit elderly residents at home to link them with appropriate community services.
- **Community Networks for Seniors (CNS):** Helps seniors living alone or with limited support to stay safely in the community through regular check-ins and service referrals.
- **Caregiver support:** Training programmes, support groups, and resources for family caregivers.

## Polyclinics

Singapore's 27 polyclinics are the backbone of primary healthcare for older adults. They provide affordable, comprehensive care close to your home.

**Services at polyclinics:**
- Chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol)
- Health screening (Screen for Life programme)
- Vaccinations (influenza, pneumococcal)
- Mental health services
- Dental care
- Physiotherapy and occupational therapy
- Eye care
- Podiatry (foot care)
- Pharmacy services and medication counselling
- Referrals to specialist outpatient clinics

**Tips for polyclinic visits:**
- Book appointments in advance through the HealthHub app or website.
- Bring your NRIC, any referral letters, and all your current medications.
- Write down your questions before the visit so you do not forget.
- Ask for a longer appointment if you have multiple concerns.

## Healthcare Subsidies and Financial Schemes

Singapore's healthcare system has multiple layers of financial support. Understanding these schemes can significantly reduce your healthcare costs.

### Community Health Assist Scheme (CHAS)

CHAS provides subsidies for medical and dental care at participating GP clinics and dental clinics.

**Who is eligible:**
All Singapore citizens are eligible for CHAS. The level of subsidy depends on your household income and the type of housing you live in.

**Card types and benefits:**
- **CHAS Blue:** For lower-income households. Highest subsidies for both acute and chronic conditions.
- **CHAS Orange:** For lower-middle-income households. Moderate subsidies.
- **CHAS Green:** For all other Singapore citizen households. Subsidies for selected chronic conditions.

**How to apply:**
Apply online through the CHAS website, at any community club (CC), or at participating CHAS clinics. You will need your NRIC and address details.

### Pioneer Generation Package

If you are a Singapore citizen born on or before 31 December 1949 and obtained citizenship on or before 31 December 1986, you are a Pioneer Generation member.

**Benefits include:**
- Annual MediSave top-ups of $200 to $800 (depending on age)
- Additional 50% off subsidised bills at specialist outpatient clinics
- Subsidies of up to $25 per visit at CHAS GP clinics (on top of existing CHAS subsidies)
- Additional Medisave for MediShield Life premiums

### Merdeka Generation Package

If you are a Singapore citizen born between 1 January 1950 and 31 December 1959 and obtained citizenship on or before 31 December 1996, you are a Merdeka Generation member.

**Benefits include:**
- Annual MediSave top-ups of $100 to $350 (depending on age)
- Additional 25% off subsidised bills at specialist outpatient clinics
- Additional subsidies at CHAS GP and dental clinics
- Additional MediSave for MediShield Life premiums

### MediSave

MediSave is part of your Central Provident Fund (CPF) specifically for healthcare expenses. As an older adult, you can use MediSave for:
- Hospitalisation and day surgery
- Outpatient treatment for selected chronic diseases under the Chronic Disease Management Programme (CDMP), including diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, stroke, and asthma
- Vaccinations recommended under the National Adult Immunisation Schedule
- Health screening under the Screen for Life programme

### MediShield Life

MediShield Life is a basic health insurance plan that covers large hospital bills and selected costly outpatient treatments. All Singapore citizens and permanent residents are automatically covered.

For older adults, premiums are higher but government premium subsidies help keep costs manageable. Pioneer and Merdeka Generation members receive additional premium support.

### MediFund

MediFund is the government's safety net for patients who cannot afford their medical bills even after using MediSave and MediShield Life. It is available at all public healthcare institutions.

**How to apply:** Speak to the medical social worker at the hospital or polyclinic. They will assess your eligibility and help you apply.

### ElderFund

ElderFund helps elderly patients who require long-term care at nursing homes, chronic sick hospitals, or inpatient palliative care.

### Seniors' Mobility and Enabling Fund (SMF)

SMF provides subsidies for:
- Assistive devices (wheelchairs, hearing aids, walking aids)
- Home modifications (grab bars, ramps)
- Transport for medical appointments

## Home and Community Care Services

If you or your loved one needs help at home, several types of services are available:

### Home Medical Services
Doctors visit your home for regular health assessments and management of chronic conditions. Suitable for those who have difficulty travelling to a clinic.

### Home Nursing
Trained nurses provide wound care, medication management, catheter care, and other nursing procedures at home.

### Home Therapy
Physiotherapists, occupational therapists, and speech therapists visit your home to provide rehabilitation and exercises.

### Home Personal Care
Care workers assist with daily activities such as bathing, dressing, feeding, and toileting.

### Meals on Wheels
Hot meals delivered to homebound elderly who are unable to prepare meals. Several organisations offer this service at subsidised rates.

### Medical Escort and Transport
Trained escorts accompany elderly patients to and from medical appointments. Subsidised transport is also available for those with mobility challenges.

**How to access these services:**
Contact AIC Silver Line at 1800-650-6060, or ask your doctor or medical social worker for a referral.

## Day Care and Rehabilitation

### Senior Care Centres (Day Care)
These centres provide day-time supervision, activities, meals, and some therapy for older adults who need support during the day. They allow seniors to continue living at home while receiving the care they need, and provide respite for caregivers.

### Community Rehabilitation
Short-term rehabilitation programmes after hospital discharge or for ongoing conditions. Physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy are provided at community locations close to home.

## Health Screening

Regular health screening can detect problems early, when they are most treatable.

### Screen for Life
A national health screening programme for Singapore citizens and permanent residents aged 18 and above. Recommended screenings for older adults include:
- Diabetes (blood sugar)
- Hypertension (blood pressure)
- High cholesterol
- Colorectal cancer
- Cervical cancer (women)
- Breast cancer (women aged 50 and above)

Screenings are available at polyclinics and CHAS GP clinics with subsidies. Most screenings cost $5 or less for eligible Singaporeans.

### Functional Screening
The AIC and polyclinics offer functional screening to assess mobility, strength, and fall risk. This can help identify early signs of frailty.

## Vaccinations

The National Adult Immunisation Schedule recommends the following for older adults:
- **Influenza vaccine:** Yearly, especially for those with chronic conditions
- **Pneumococcal vaccine:** To protect against pneumonia (one dose, with a booster after 5 years for some)
- **Shingles vaccine:** For those aged 50 and above (not subsidised but recommended)

Vaccinations are available at polyclinics and participating CHAS GP clinics, with MediSave claimable for recommended vaccines.
