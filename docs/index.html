The Problem

  • 1 in 3 Singaporeans aged 65+ fall each year, often leading to hospitalisation and loss of independence
  • Frailty screening requires clinic visits — many seniors skip them due to mobility barriers
  • Physiotherapy is expensive and inaccessible for the 900,000+ residents aged 65+
  • Caregivers lack real-time visibility into their loved one's mobility decline

Our Solution

  • Phone-camera SPPB assessment using pose estimation — no wearables, no clinic visits
  • AI-personalised exercise plans adapted to frailty tier and specific mobility deficits
  • Multilingual chat companion (English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil) with Singlish-aware voice
  • Caregiver dashboard with automated safety alerts for falls and emergencies
